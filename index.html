Project 01 · Smart pen

A pen that knows what the hand is doing

A hardware client came to us with a pen and a question: can its gyroscope and other sensors tell us what's being written? We built the answer - reading the sensors at the source and running the motion through a custom AI pipeline that turns raw angular movement into clean, usable stroke data.

Project 02 · Asset tracking

Every asset on one live map

For a client we designed and built a comprehensive tracking interface: assets live on the map with position, heading and status readable at control-room distance. The miniature here is a taste - click anywhere on it and the unit computes its heading and goes.
